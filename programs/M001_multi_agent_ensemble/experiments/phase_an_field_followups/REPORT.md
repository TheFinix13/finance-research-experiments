## What the failures teach (mechanism, not spin)

1. **Squad context inflates survey KPIs.** Rin:USDJPY was the AL A1
   headline (n=336, PF 1.212 in squad context) and is PF 0.924 with
   0/5 stability in isolation. In the squad replay his USDJPY flow was
   filtered by contests, sentinel slots, and peer presence; alone he
   fires ~454 trades and the incremental ~120 are toxic. Same story,
   smaller, for Bachira:NZDUSD (survey 1.269 → isolation 1.078) and
   Chigiri:AUDUSD (survey 1.376 → isolation 0.994, path-unstable).
   **Registry note: survey (squad-context) nominations must be
   discounted; isolation is the weapon's true expectancy.** Deployed
   performance sits somewhere between the two, because deployment IS
   squad context — but a cell that fails isolation this badly has no
   claim to a roster slot on that field.
2. **Barou:USDJPY is a real near-miss, not a rounding excuse.** PF
   1.138 [1.113–1.161] with mean R +0.105 (2x the floor) and 5/5
   stability on n=418 — his largest honest sample ever, answering his
   n-growth charter: the rare-fire pattern was a field problem, not a
   weapon problem. The PF floor (1.15) is final and it FAILED. The
   legitimate path forward is a Barou v1.x balance patch (one
   mechanism change, new pre-reg, fresh floors) — not a re-judgment.
3. **Barou's home field (USDCAD) fails in isolation** (0.957, 1/5).
   His home-survey numbers were squad-context artifacts; his best
   solo fields are trending ones (USDJPY, and USTEC holds 5/5 PF > 1
   but dies at honest index costs).
4. **Chigiri travels only to silver.** The AUDUSD nomination died in
   isolation; XAGUSD survived everything. Two independent lenses had
   already said his high-conviction core is sound (AK-2 mirrors,
   AM survey); the sealed pass is the third and strongest.

## Multiplicity, settled

Only ONE sealed open occurred (not five), so the family's false-pass
exposure is the single-open case: if chigiri:XAGUSD had no edge, the

verdict is therefore **validated-with-single-pass-caveat**: strong
enough to act on (paper-first), not strong enough to call proven.
Confirmation comes only from the live/paper tape going forward.
