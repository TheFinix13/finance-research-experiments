## TL;DR

* **Predicate starvation: fixed.** Nagi went from 0 confluence thoughts
  in Φ4 to **34302** in Φ4.1. The F11/F13 chemistry works. The
  expanded roster (Bachira, Rin, Chigiri, Reo) delivers the
  shared-tag × overlapping-coordinate × matching-direction
  combinations that Φ4 lacked. We can close this open question.
* **Squad gate: STILL FAIL.** Ratio 0.92x. The fix introduced a NEW
  failure mode: **structural crowding-out**. Isagi traded 0 times.
  Barou traded 0 times. Every Isagi/Barou proposal lost the
  aggregator to a higher-conviction Bachira / Rin / Chigiri
  proposal. The squad's effective composition collapsed to "mostly
  Bachira" -- 2840 of 3714 trades (76%) are Bachira.
* **Φ5 lever is the aggregator, not more strikers.** Adding more
  agents will deepen the crowd-out. The fix is per-agent risk
  budgeting (HRP) + same-direction merge in the aggregator +
  TQS-conditional conviction lifts.

## What the auto-report misses

The auto-report's diagnosis says "YES" to "did predicate starvation
get fixed" and stops. The three observations below reshape what the
Φ5 plan should look like:

### 1. Isagi made 0 trades. Barou made 0 trades.

Φ3 baseline: Isagi alone made ~50 trades on EURUSD over the dev
window, +11.04 median pips, TQS 0.317.

Φ4: Isagi made ~60 trades, Barou made ~25 trades. The aggregator's
per-symbol highest-conviction-wins rule worked fine -- Isagi's 0.65
base conviction was the only EURUSD proposal most ticks, so he won.

Φ4.1: Isagi made **0 trades**. Barou made **0 trades**. Why?

* **Bachira** fires the SAME baseline-zone primitive that Isagi
  uses, on the SAME three symbols, but with a +0.10 rebel lift to
  0.75 conviction whenever the recent-opposite-swing trigger fires.
* **Rin** fires the SAME zone_d1_against gate as Isagi on EURUSD
  with a +0.15 precision lift to 0.80 whenever the R:R ≥ 2.5
  + ≥ 20-pip stop filter passes.

On every tick where Isagi proposes, at least one of Bachira / Rin
proposes too at strictly higher conviction. The aggregator picks the
higher-conviction proposal and Isagi's proposal lands on the
rejected-proposals JSONL. Same dynamic crowds Barou out on USDCAD.

This is **structural crowding-out**, not a bug. It is the direct
mechanical consequence of how we engineered the predicate-starvation
fix (conviction lifts to clear the 0.70 Nagi floor). The fix worked
at the chemistry level (Nagi fires 34302 times); it MISFIRED at
the execution layer (the baseline cell never gets to trade).

**Evidence:** `reviews/phi41_isagi_rejection_analysis.md` -- 5657
Isagi rejections in "same-direction" bucket (87.5%), 914 in
"opposite-direction" (12.5%), 0 in "silent", 0 in "elsewhere". The
same-direction dominance is the fingerprint of crowd-out: Isagi's
edge is being USED (peers fire the same direction on the same tick)
but ATTRIBUTED to the higher-conviction agent.

### 2. Nagi fired 34302 confluence thoughts but only 94 trades

Ratio: ~365 confluence thoughts per actual trade.

The per-symbol single-position rule (max 1 open trade per symbol at
a time) blocks Nagi's `intend()` whenever any peer has already
opened on that symbol. Nagi is structurally last-to-trade because
his thought reads peers at T-1 and is only consulted after the
aggregator has already picked a winner. By the time Nagi's
confluence trigger fires, the open-position concurrency limit has
kicked in.

But: the 94 Nagi trades show **+10.28 mean pips / TQS 0.349** --
the **best per-trade quality** in the squad. When Nagi DOES get a
slot, his trades are good. The aggregator + concurrency limit is
denying him airtime, not denying him edge.

### 3. Squad TQS drops because the trade mix is now noisier

Φ3 (Isagi alone): single primitive, single direction-gate, single
agent's view -> TQS 0.317.

Φ4.1 (8 agents): six different decision pathways (baseline zone
with no D1 gate, zone_d1_against base, zone_d1_against precision
lift, ATR breakout, confluence on prior-tick peers, baseline zone
USDCAD-only), all running through the same per-symbol aggregator and
concurrency rule. Resulting trade ledger:

| `bachira_meguru` | 2840 | +9.97 | +14.21 | 0.308 | 50.9% |

Squad mean TQS = trade-weighted blend of these = 0.292. Bachira
dominates the mix at 76% by trade count and her standalone TQS

(0.229) and Rin (0.277) drag the squad below the Isagi baseline.
That's the entire arithmetic of the 0.92x ratio.

**The Φ4.1 squad's TQS = weighted average TQS of whichever agents
the aggregator lets trade.** Today the aggregator's selection is
biased toward the highest-conviction agent, which is the +0.10
rebel-lifted Bachira -- so the squad's effective composition is
"mostly Bachira" -- and Bachira's standalone TQS is the ceiling of
what we can achieve with this aggregator + crowd-out dynamic.

## What this tells us about Φ5

The Φ4.1 FAIL is a **different failure** from Φ4's FAIL.

| Phase | Failure mode | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Φ4 | Predicate starvation: Nagi never fires | FAIL 0.98x |
| Φ4.1 | Predicate fires, but aggregator + concurrency crowd out baseline cell | FAIL 0.92x |

The right Φ5 lever is the **aggregator**, not another striker:

* **Per-agent risk budget** (HRP allocator, `09 §1.5` G5
  talk-track). Rather than picking the single highest-conviction
  proposal per symbol, allocate notional across all same-direction
  proposals weighted by their TQS estimate AND their
  inverse-correlation with the squad. This gives Isagi back some
  airtime while keeping the predicate-starvation fix.
* **TQS-conditional conviction floor.** Don't accept Bachira's
  +0.10 rebel lift unconditionally -- gate it on the agent's
  trailing 30-trade TQS estimate. Bachira (0.308) already passes;
  Rin (0.277) and Chigiri (0.229) would not. This is a clean way
  to filter out conviction lifts that don't earn their keep, and
  has the right interpretation: an agent has to PROVE its lift is
  worth listening to.
* **Same-direction merge** in the aggregator. When multiple peers
  propose the same direction on the same symbol, don't pick one;
  merge them into a single proposal whose conviction is the F11
  chemical-reaction lift `1 - prod(1 - c_i)` and whose entry / stop
  is the inverse-variance blend. This is the original
  `sim/core/aggregator.py` semantics that the Φ4 harness OVERRODE
  for the headline-conviction comparison; Φ5 should restore it.

A roster expansion to A8 Yukimiya, A9 Aoshi, or A1 Isagi v2 will
not fix Φ4.1's failure mode -- it will probably make it worse by
adding more agents to compete with Isagi at the aggregator. The Φ5
deliverable needs to be aggregator + risk allocator, not more
strikers.

## F17 ΔInfo: not interpretable in this run

Every F17 row reports ΔInfo +0.000 with a CI that collapses on
zero. Three structural reasons:

1. We sampled only 1 of 7 OOS windows for compute economy
   (`--delta-info-windows 1`).
2. The TQS distribution is fat-tailed with a long zero-tqs left arm
   (a losing trade gets TQS 0 in F12); medians on a single OOS
   window are dominated by zeros.
3. Reo + Barou + Nagi isolated arms have 0 trades by construction
   or near-zero trades due to crowd-out, so their bootstrap CIs are
   meaningless.

The F17 results are **not interpretable** in this run. A proper F17
measurement needs more OOS windows AND a TQS aggregation that
weighs by trade count, not by median per window. Out of scope for
Φ4.1; this is a Φ5 methodology fix.
