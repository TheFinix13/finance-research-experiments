## §0 Provenance and taint declaration

E028 (stopped dead at Stage 1, 2026-07-28) found the Power-of-Three
reversal narrative **inverted** on EURUSD M15 2015–2021: after London
takes exactly one side of the Asia range, NY completes to the opposite
extreme only 26.2 % of the time (vs 61.2 % matched baseline) and
extends beyond London's own extreme 60.2 % of the time. E030
pre-registers the mirror-image hypothesis: **NY drifts in London's
take direction on one-side days.**

**Taint:** the hypothesis was generated by looking at EURUSD M15
2015–2021 (E028's screen). That slice can lock effect sizes but can
never confirm. The inferential verdict starts at Stage 2 on data this
statistic has never been computed on. E028's *reversal-trade*
outcomes on 2015–2021 do not constitute a prior computation of the
*continuation-drift* statistic, but the directional information
overlaps — hence the demotion of Stage 1 to a non-claiming
effect-size lock.

## §1 Hypothesis and mechanical rule

On one-side London days (class `HIGH_ONLY` or `LOW_ONLY` per E028's
frozen classifier):

| Component | Rule |
|---|---|
| Arm LONG (cell 1) | `HIGH_ONLY` (London broke Asia highs) → **long**, continuation |
| Arm SHORT (cell 2) | `LOW_ONLY` → **short**, continuation |
| Entry | Close of the first M15 bar with open time ≥ 13:30 UTC |
| Exit | Close of the last M15 bar before 21:00 UTC — **time exit only; no SL, no TP** |
| Costs | 0.3 pip/side base; 1.0 pip/side stress (both reported) |

Zero geometry knobs by design: the claim is *directional drift*, and
every bracket parameter would be a degree of freedom the taint budget
cannot afford.

**H0.** Mean net pips per trade ≤ 0 at base costs.
**H1.** Mean net pips > 0 at base costs (bootstrap, one-sided) AND
mean > 0 at stress costs.

**Placebo contrast (reported, feeds Stage-1 stop):** the same
13:30→21:00 drift measured in the *London-take direction of the day's
class* cannot be defined on BOTH/NEITHER days; instead both directed
drifts are reported on BOTH and NEITHER days as placebo arms — if
one-side-day drift is not distinguishably better than placebo-day
drift in the same direction, the class conveys nothing.

**Verdict per cell (locked, from Stage 2 on).** `alive` iff
n ≥ 100 AND bootstrap 95 % CI lower bound > 0 at base costs AND
mean > 0 at stress costs AND stage-threshold significant
(`p = (1 + #{resample mean ≤ 0}) / (1 + B)`, B = 10,000).
`parked_weak_effect` iff n ≥ 100 and mean > 0 at base costs but any
other condition fails. `parked_insufficient_n` iff n < 100. Otherwise
`dead`.

## §4 Stop rules (pre-declared)

Stops emit `stage{N}_E030_stop.json` under `output/E030_london_continuation/`.

- **Stage 1 (go/no-go).** If either cell's mean net pips at base costs
  ≤ 0 on 2015–2021, OR the one-side drift does not exceed the matched
  placebo-day drift, the E028-inversion reading is falsified in-sample
  → **STOP before any out-of-sample data is touched.** Sealed
  reservation released (E029's co-reservation unaffected).
- **Stage 2.** 0/2 alive → **STOP**; cells tagged per §1; downstream
  slices unconsumed; sealed reservation released.
- **Stage 3.** 0 replicate → EURUSD-local; do not promote; sealed
  reservation released.
- **Stage 4.** Failure → publish as null, `dead` at sealed level.
