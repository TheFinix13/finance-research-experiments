\title{Do Classical Price-Action Events Carry Directional Information?\\
Test A of a Role-Structured Confluence Study}
\author{Fiyin Akano}
\date{June 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Traders often claim that overlapping technical patterns---``confluence''---
produce stronger price reactions than isolated signals. We pre-registered and
executed Test A of a staged screening study: 76 price-action event types
across four timeframes (284 cells) on EURUSD (2015--2021 screen split).
A critical methodological finding emerged: uniform random-time controls were
invalid on M15 due to session-volatility confounding (random forward movement
varied 3.7$\times$ by hour-of-day). After amendment to hour-matched controls,
five cells survived screening (all M15). One confirmed on frozen 2022--2024
data (\texttt{trendline\_liquidity\_sweep\_low}, +0.30 ATR effect); one
replicated on frozen GBPUSD (\texttt{channel\_top\_touch}). Effects are
real but small---suitable as gate inputs, not standalone strategies. This
repository is observation-only and does not trade.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Research question}
\begin{quote}
\emph{H$_0$}: Conditional on a classical price-action event, ATR-normalised
directional forward excursion is indistinguishable from direction- and
time-matched random baselines.

\emph{H$_1$}: Specific (timeframe $\times$ event type) cells beat that
baseline; specific cross-timeframe combinations beat their parts.
\end{quote}

This is Test A (price action only) of a three-family program (Tests B:
indicators; C: cross-family interactions). Test A tests each method
individually by timeframe before any combination claims.

\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Event dictionary (Stage 0)}
Eighteen detector modules emit 76 causal event types: market structure
(BOS/CHoCH), zones/order blocks/breakers, fair-value gaps, liquidity pools
and sweeps, trendlines/channels, horizontal S/R, chart patterns, fibonacci
tags, and candlestick patterns. Each event carries a pre-registered
directional hypothesis (+1 up / $-$1 down). The dictionary was frozen before
any statistics were computed.

\subsection{Outcome metric}
For an event at bar $t$: maximum favourable excursion (MFE) in the event
direction over horizon $H$ (D1: 30, H4/H1: 20, M15: 16 bars), divided by
ATR(14); plus binary hit = reaches +1 ATR before $-$1 ATR.

\subsection{Controls and amendment v2.1}
Pre-registered controls: random bar indices with directions resampled from
the event mix. This produced 41/284 ``significant'' cells---including
mutually contradictory hypotheses---indicating a broken null. Diagnostic
analysis showed MFE at random M15 times varies from 1.09 ATR (19:00 UTC) to
4.03 ATR (05:00 UTC) because ATR(14) lags the intraday session cycle.

\textbf{Amendment v2.1} (documented before re-run): controls matched to each
event's hour-of-day and direction. The invalid uniform run is retained as a
cautionary record.

\subsection{Statistics}
Permutation test (2{,}000 shuffles), one-sided; Benjamini--Hochberg FDR at
5\% across adequately powered cells ($n \geq 100$). Four-tier verdicts:
\texttt{alive}, \texttt{parked\_weak\_effect}, \texttt{parked\_insufficient\_n},
\texttt{dead}.

\subsection{Out-of-sample confirmation}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Confirm split}: \texttt{trendline\_liquidity\_sweep\_low}
        confirmed (+0.308 ATR, $p=0.0065$).
  \item \textbf{GBPUSD replication}: \texttt{channel\_top\_touch} replicated
        (+0.099 ATR, $p=0.0005$).
  \item All five survivors positive on both OOS tests (sign-consistency
        $\approx$3\% under random-sign null).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Stage 2}
Strict Stage 2 (alive cells only) was \textbf{empty by construction}---no
higher-timeframe survivor to serve as context. Exploratory Stage 2 (including
parked-weak cells) found most ``context $\times$ setup'' lift explained by
setup marginals; H1 \texttt{equal\_highs\_pool} context flagged for
pre-registered Stage-2b.

\section{Discussion}

The session-volatility confound is a general lesson for intraday FX research:
ATR-normalised metrics on M15 require time-matched nulls. Surviving effects
(+0.05 to +0.35 ATR MFE) are too small for standalone M15 strategies after
spread but may inform confluence gating or exit logic in a validated main
agent---only via that agent's separate pipeline.

\section{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Detector definitions are one auditable formalisation per concept.
  \item D1 cells are structurally underpowered at $n \geq 100$ per pair.
  \item MFE measures opportunity, not net P\&L; costs not modelled.
  \item Tests B and C not yet pre-registered.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
Classical price-action confluence is \textbf{partially supported} on M15
trendline/channel geometry, not broadly across the full dictionary. The
honest headline: a handful of modest, reproducible signals---not the
``everything works on M15'' illusion the broken first analysis suggested.
This lab never trades; findings are hypothesis-generating for external
validation.
