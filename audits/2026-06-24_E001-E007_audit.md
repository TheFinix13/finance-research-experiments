## 2. Per-experiment audit

The per-experiment quality assessment uses these axes:

- **Pre-registration:** was the protocol committed before any outcome was scored?
- **Splits / OOS hygiene:** were screen / confirm / sealed splits defined and respected?
- **Multiple-testing correction:** was BH-FDR (or equivalent) applied across the family?
- **Stop rules:** were stop conditions pre-declared and obeyed?
- **Negative-result discipline:** were dead cells reported with the same prominence as alive ones?

### 2.1 E001 — ICT concept ablation

- **Folder:** `experiments/E001_concept_ablation/`
- **Code (production):** `agent/alphas/grid.py`, `agent/alphas/concepts/*` in the agent repo (no lab-side code)
- **Pre-reg commit:** none (executed-then-registered at `d2f034b` on 2026-06-16)
- **Execution dates:** 2026-06-09 to 2026-06-10 in `multi-pair-trading-agent`

**Hypothesis.** Each ICT concept (FVG retest, BOS continuation, order blocks, fib OTE, momentum, liquidity sweep, supply/demand zone), tested *alone* across `TF × session` grids on EURUSD 2015–2025, produces positive expectancy after bootstrap p-values and BH-FDR 5%.

**Methodology in 2–4 lines.** One alpha per run through `agent/alphas/grid.py:AblationCell`, with TF ∈ {D1, H4, H1, M15, M5} × session ∈ {all, london, ny, london_ny_overlap, asia}. Bootstrap p per cell, BH-FDR 5% across the full grid, fixed 0.3 pip/side cost. A "fair-shot" second wave relaxed entry for momentum/liquidity-sweep.

**Status.** COMPLETED.

**Key findings.**
- 6 of 7 ICT concepts (FVG, BOS, order blocks, fib OTE, momentum, liquidity sweep) failed BH-FDR on every cell.
- Supply/demand zone touch survived. Subsequent post-ablation discovery: fading the zone *against* a D1 trend (`zone_d1_against`, htf_align=D1/mode=against/lookback=10/min_move_pips=60) strengthened the edge; *with*-trend gating destroyed it.
- This is the founding "the retail vocabulary is over-applied" result for the whole program. The only thing carried forward was `zone_d1_against`.

**Quality assessment.**
- Pre-registration: **no** (retrospective, disclosed). Carries the standard funnel-selection caveat.
- Splits / OOS hygiene: in-sample only; the funnel selected on the same window later used for holdout (E003) and walk-forward (E004). E003 and E004 were designed explicitly to address this selection bias.
- Multiple-testing correction: BH-FDR 5% across the 25 cells per concept and across concepts. Adequate.
- Negative-result discipline: 6/7 concepts reported as eliminated with the same prominence as the survivor.
- **Verdict:** Sound for what it is. The retrospective tag and the explicit downstream selection-bias control (E003/E004) keep it honest. The lab REPORT.md and MANIFEST.md correctly say "code stays in agent; lab does not duplicate raw results."

**Recommendation: KEEP-ON-MAIN-AS-ARCHIVED.**

**Relevance to M001.**
- **Direct.** The `zone_d1_against` parameters this funnel produced are the seed for **A1 Isagi v1** (`programs/M001_multi_agent_ensemble/05-agent-roster-v0.md` §3.1). The doctrine note in `05` says explicitly that Isagi v1 "wraps existing `agent/alphas/concepts/zone_alpha.py`".
- **Indirect.** The negative results on FVG/OB/BOS/fib OTE/momentum/liquidity-sweep are *priors* for the M001 roster: anywhere an agent re-invokes one of these primitives (Bachira on patterns, Rin on fib, A1 Isagi v2 on FVG/OB in `05-agent-roster-v0.md` §3.1 "v2 adds full IRL/ERL/FVG/OB"), the prior should be that the standalone edge is at best small.
- **Caveat.** E001 tested these concepts *alone*; the M001 frame (late fusion, confluence) is explicitly about combination. E006 partially re-tested them as marginals on a richer dictionary and got the same answer for everything except a small M15 fib/trendline/channel residue.

### 2.3 E003 — Holdout validation

- **Folder:** `experiments/E003_holdout_validation/`
- **Code (production):** `multi-pair-trading-agent/scripts/run_holdout_validation.py`
- **Pre-reg commit:** none (retrospective)
- **Execution date:** 2026-06-10

**Hypothesis.** IS-significant cells from E002 repeat positive expectancy on a single OOS holdout (IS: 2015-01 → 2022-12; OOS: 2023-01 → 2025-12).

**Methodology in 2–4 lines.** Took the 8 strongest E002 cells by IS expectancy; ran each on the held-out 2023–2025 window; verdict = "validated" if OOS mean positive and bootstrap-significant at 5%. Same fixed-cost model as E001/E002.

**Status.** COMPLETED.

**Key findings.**
- **1 of 8** validated OOS: `zone_d1_against / H4 / asia`.
- 7/8 collapsed; the largest collapse was a D1 cell going from +25 IS pips/trade to +1 OOS pip/trade.
- **The Asia survivor was itself an artefact of one OOS window** — E004's walk-forward later showed the per-trade edge on H4-all-sessions is statistically indistinguishable from H4-asia and uses ~4× the sample. So the *interesting lesson is the limit of single-split holdout*, not the headline survivor.

**Quality assessment.**
- Pre-registration: no (retrospective).

- Negative-result discipline: the 7/8 collapse rate is reported with the same prominence as the survivor; the D1 +25 → +1 collapse is called out as the cleanest illustration of selection-bias risk.
- **Verdict:** The methodology is *intentionally limited* and the report names the limit. Acceptable as long as E004 is treated as the controlling result.

**Recommendation: KEEP-ON-MAIN-AS-ARCHIVED.** The selection-bias lesson is more valuable than the headline.

**Relevance to M001.**
- M001's research standards (`07-research-standards.md` per the index in `programs/M001_multi_agent_ensemble/README.md`) call for *nested walk-forward CV*, not single-split holdout. E003 is the cautionary precedent that justifies that choice. **It is worth quoting verbatim in M001's standards doc when explaining why nested WF rather than single split.**

### 2.4 E004 — Walk-forward validation

- **Folder:** `experiments/E004_walk_forward/` (with lab copies of agent reports under `results/`)
- **Code (production):** `multi-pair-trading-agent/scripts/run_walk_forward.py`, `scripts/analyze_walk_forward.py`
- **Pre-reg commit:** none (retrospective)
- **Execution date:** 2026-06-09

**Hypothesis.** Robust cells produce positive OOS expectancy in most rolling windows, not just one holdout slice. Pre-declared bar: ≥ 4 of 7 windows positive.

**Methodology in 2–4 lines.** Seven rolling 4-year-IS / 1-year-OOS windows on EURUSD 2015–2025. Parameters locked. Compared `zone_d1_against / H4 / asia` (the E003 survivor) against `zone_d1_against / H4 / all sessions`.

**Status.** COMPLETED.

**Key findings.**
- `zone_d1_against / H4 / all`: **7 of 7** OOS windows positive, median **+11.34 pips/trade**, ~66 trades/window.
- `zone_d1_against / H4 / asia`: 7/7 positive, +11.36 pips/trade, ~15 trades/window. Same per-trade edge, one-quarter the sample.
- The deployment cell was switched from `H4/asia` (E003's apparent survivor) to `H4/all` on sample-size grounds. This is the moment the production agent's cell was chosen.
- A coin-flip null would put 7/7 at $2^{-7} \approx 0.008$. This is the cleanest single piece of evidence in the program.

**Quality assessment.**
- Pre-registration: no (retrospective).
- Splits / OOS hygiene: nested rolling windows — exactly the right design for the question.
- Multiple-testing correction: not formally an FDR family because the question is about *consistency*, not per-window α. The 7/7 sign test functions as the multiple-test statistic.
- Negative-result discipline: the lab report includes the comparison with the Asia-only cell and the explicit note that the Asia restriction was selection bias, not a real session-specific edge.
- **Verdict:** Methodologically strong. Retrospective registration is the only knock; even without pre-registration, walk-forward is much harder to overfit than ablation or single-split holdout.

**Recommendation: PROMOTE-TO-MAIN-DOCS** (specifically: the *result* — 7/7 OOS, median +11.34 — and the *methodology lesson* that walk-forward dominated single-split holdout and changed the deployment choice). Suggested location: `docs/findings/2026-06-09_walk_forward_validation.md` (a new short docs entry summarising the deployed cell and pointing at the experiment folder). Keep the experiment folder itself archived as-is.

**Relevance to M001.**
- **Direct.** This is what `A1 Isagi v1` is. Any M001 agent that ever wants to graduate to production must produce evidence of comparable strength on its own rule. The C1 promotion gate (`programs/M001_multi_agent_ensemble/00-charter.md` §"Success criteria") references the agent-side validation chain that E004 instantiates.
- **Direct.** The 11.34 pips/trade median is the **baseline** the M001 ensemble must beat by ≥ 10 % on TQS per C1, on the same sealed 2026 H1 data.

### 2.7 E007 — Impulse-origin bounce (Test B)

- **Folder:** `experiments/E007_impulse_origin_bounce/`
- **Code (lab):** `conflab/detectors_impulse_return.py`, `conflab/friction.py`, `scripts/test_b/run_stage1.py`, `run_stage2.py`, `run_stage3.py`, `run_stage4_friction.py`, `render_figures.py`, `_lib.py`
- **Pre-reg commit:** `b9715d9` (2026-06-16; "Pre-register Test B")
- **Detector commit:** `5bc0145` ("Test B: detector + friction module + unit tests (no data run yet)")
- **Amendment commit:** `2ae4aee` ("Test B Stage 1: amendment 6.2 + canonical 0.50 run + cautionary 0.30 record")
- **Close commit:** `386b4cb` ("Test B closed: H1 dies at Stage 1 (0/12 alive, BH-FDR α=0.05)")
- **Outputs:** `output/test_b/stage1_*.jsonl`, `output/test_b/figures/fig{1..5}_*.png`, `stage{2,3,4}_*.stop.json`

**Hypothesis.** (H1) A return to the origin zone of a strong, clean impulse leg on EURUSD H4/H1 produces higher MFE in the impulse direction than at hour-matched random levels with the same direction. (H2, conditional on H1) Low-friction return paths amplify the H1 effect. (H3, conditional on H1+H2) H1 replicates on GBPUSD/USDCAD on a sealed cross-pair look. **The "always bounces" framing tested as a measurable conditional probability.**

**Methodology in 2–4 lines.** Pre-registered family of 12 cells: 2 TFs (H4, H1) × 2 directions (up, down) × 3 impulse-size thresholds (1.0, 1.5, 2.0 ATR). Stage-1 permutation test (5,000 shuffles), BH-FDR 5% across 12 cells, n_gate=30. Stop rule: if 0 alive at Stage 1 → STOP. Amendment 6.2 (pre-MFE, count-only diagnostic): relaxed `max_retrace_frac` from 0.30 to 0.50 because 0.30 gave 1–4 candidate legs per cell (below n_gate). 30% record preserved as cautionary file.

**Status.** COMPLETED. Stopped at Stage 1 by the protocol's own stop rule. Stages 2–4 did not run (recorded as `stage{2,3,4}_..._stop.json`).

**Key findings.**

## 3. Cross-experiment patterns

After reading all seven, the following lessons keep recurring.

### 3.1 Consistent strengths

- **Negative results are the norm, and they are reported.** E001 killed 6/7 ICT concepts; E006 killed 186/284 cells outright (plus 77 parked-insufficient-n and 16 parked-weak); E007 killed all 12. In every case the negative is reported with the same prominence as the positive. This is unusual in trading research and is the lab's biggest discipline asset.
- **Selection-bias awareness is real.** E001 → E003 → E004 is a deliberate funnel-to-walk-forward progression. E003's apparent survivor (`H4 / asia`) was correctly identified as a single-window artefact in E004 and the deployment cell was changed. The lab does not protect its own past claims.
- **OOS hygiene is taken seriously.** Sealed splits are sealed (E005 sealed 2026, E006 sealed 2025+). Cross-pair frozen tests use zero re-tuning (E005). The `DATA_LEDGER.md` tracks `(pair, TF, slice)` usage so a future hypothesis can't honestly claim a slice as unseen if it was used.
- **Compute-vs-claim is operative.** E006 and E007 compute statistics for *every* cell regardless of n; the n_gate only governs which cells are eligible to be called `alive`. This is the right way to avoid "the cells we cared about" bias.

### 3.2 Recurring failure modes — and the fixes that landed

- **Single-OOS-window selection bias** (E003). Fixed by walk-forward (E004) and treated as a binding methodological lesson.
- **Random-control mis-specification on intraday TFs** (E006 v2.0). Uniform-time controls were not exchangeable with events because ATR(14) lagged the intraday session cycle and produced a 3.7× hour-of-day variation in random MFE. **Fixed pre-MFE** by amendment v2.1 (hour-matched controls), with the uniform-control registry preserved. This is the most important methodological discovery in the program and now binds every intraday experiment via `PROTOCOL_DISCIPLINE.md` §3.
- **Infeasibility from over-strict locked parameters** (E007 v0). The pre-registered `max_retrace_frac = 0.30` produced 1–4 candidate legs per cell — below the n_gate before the return-touch filter even ran. **Fixed pre-MFE** by amendment 6.2 (relaxed to 0.50, the smallest value that crossed n=30), with the 30% record preserved as cautionary. Amendment workflow itself is now a reusable template.
- **Effect-size honesty.** E006 survivors have effects of +0.05 to +0.35 ATR of MFE with hit-rate deltas under 2 percentage points. The report says explicitly that these are gate inputs, not standalone strategies, and that the smallest cell sits inside the EURUSD M15 spread cost band. The lab does not chase noise into deployment.

### 3.3 Systemic gaps that should affect how M001 reads this corpus

- **E001–E005 are retrospective.** The work happened, the parameters are locked in production, the discipline framework was invented after. This is honestly disclosed but means the *evidence weight* of these five experiments is lower than the same-numbered evidence would carry under pre-registration. M001's promotion gates should not treat the E001–E005 evidence as automatic permission to re-use the same parameters; only the cross-pair frozen arm (E005 Part A) is truly OOS-validated under modern discipline.
- **D1 cells are structurally underpowered.** Across E002, E003, E004, and E006, D1 cells are either parked for n, or have large point effects on small samples, or collapsed in single-split OOS. A cross-pair panel design is the right route to D1 claims and has not been done.
- **Soft data re-use on E006 screen.** The uniform-control and hour-matched runs both touched the screen split. The amendment was specified from a diagnostic that never scored MFE, and confirm/sealed data were untouched. The leakage is small, but a strict reader should treat the amended screen p-values as conditional on one analysis revision (the E006 report says so).
- **All retrospective lab claims rely on agent-side scripts that may have moved.** `agent/alphas/grid.py`, `scripts/run_holdout_validation.py`, `scripts/run_walk_forward.py`, `scripts/run_cross_pair_frozen.py` are referenced as the source of truth for E001–E005. The lab does not duplicate the code. Worth a one-off check that those paths still resolve in the current production repo (out of scope for this audit).
